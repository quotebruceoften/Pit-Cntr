    Shift briefing

      Your job

      • Dispatch the fleet. Each truck has a circuit: a loading unit and a dump (crusher, ore stockpile or waste dump). Change it in the fleet table, or select several trucks and use the bulk bar.
      • Ore goes to the crusher (or the ore stockpile if the crusher is down). Waste goes to the waste dump. A loaded truck finishes its current load before a new circuit applies; changing only the dump redirects the load on board.
      • Balance the crusher blend by mixing high-grade (HG) and low-grade (LG) ore faces.
      • Answer radio calls promptly — each has a countdown. Speed and quality both count.
      • Keep people safe. Safety failures outweigh production. A critical safety failure ends any chance of a recommendation.
